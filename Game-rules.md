# Merchants - Game rules

## About



## Components

6 different goods:
 - Sugar (White), Indigo (Blue), Spices (Red), 10x Tea (Green), 10x Silk (Purple), 10x Lumber (Brown)
 
60 good cards
 - 10x Sugar (White), 10x Indigo (Blue), 10x Spices (Red), 10x Tea (Green), 10x Silk (Purple), 10x Lumber (Brown)

30 good cubes in the 6 different goods
- 5x Sugar (White), 5x Indigo (Blue), 5x Spices (Red), 5x Tea (Green), 5x Silk (Purple), 5x Lumber (Brown)

20 Special cards:
- 14 ship cards
- 2 office cards
- 2 docker cards
- 2 trade agreement cards



> Important rule: Each ship may only ever hold 1 cube.

## Game Preparation

1. Shuffle all 60 good cards and put face down forming a draw pile.
2. Deal 3 cards to each player.  These cards are private to that player.
3. Draw 6 cards from the goods card draw pile and create a 2x3 face up into a `marketplace`.  This `marketplace` represents the current demand.
4. Give each player 2 ship cards, these are face up in front the respective player.
5. Create a common supply. Place all good cubes, coins, workers and separate the special cards into their categories, forming a common supply next to the game board.

### Preparation

Prior to game start, each player will now pick 1 cube to load each of their ships.

Cubes are drafted using a snake order.

Choose a First player.   The last player picks first, then drafting proceeds clockwise until the first player picks. The order then reverses (counter-clockwise), starting again with the last player. This continues until each player has two cubes.

Each cube drafted is placed onto a one of their available ship cards. 

## Sequence of play

Starting with the First player, and continuing clockwise, each player will become the `active player` and take a turn performing the 2 phases in sequence.

After a player has taken a turn, play continues clockwise from the First player.

The first player never changes.

## Game play

There are 2 phases, that must be done in sequence.

Each player does each phase in full, before moving to the next phase.

### Phase 1: Purchase

The active player MUST only choose 1 action in Phase 1.

- Exchange a goods cube OR,
- Buy 1 special card OR,
- Pass (do nothing)

### Phase 2: Deliver

The active player MUST either:

- Deliver: Play good cards, and trigger coin payouts 
- Draw 2 new good cards

Note: A player cannot delivery and draw 2 goods cards, they must choose.

--

### Exchanging goods cube

A player may exchange the goods cube from ONE of his ships with a different colour, from supply, if its available.

### Buy a special card

The player may spend coins to purchase a single special card, if its available and adds it in front of them (adding it to their tableau).

### Deliver: Playing good cards

The player may play any number of good cards of the SAME colour from his hand onto the goods cards in the centre of the table, thus changing the marketplace.

The marketplace is always 6 cards.

Cards played MUST played on top of existing cards in the marketplace, and may never overlay the same market index in the same delivery action.

Each card must be played on top of a different card within the marketplace.

Example: If a player makes a delivery of 2 cards, that player cannot place both cards on top of each other.

#### Triggering payout

When the active player plays cards to the marketplace via a `deliver` action, *all* players get paid out, dependent upon the colour good cubes they have on their ships.

Every player with the same colour goods cube on their ships gets 1 coin per goods cube per goods cards delivered.  

Only the cards delivered count for payout.

For example:

The marketplace has 1 blue card.  Two blue cards are delivered.  This totals 3 blue cards in the marketplace.  All players now get 3 coins per blue goods cube on their ships.

*Tom has 2 blue goods cube, and gets 3x2 = 6 coins, whereas, Sarah has 0 blue goods cube and gets nothing*

### Drawing two new good cards

The active player draws two new gods cards to their hand.

*Note: There is a hand limit of 6 cards*

## Special cards

## Ship (cost: 10 coins)
Each ship carrys one goods cube. After purchasing a ship, they must load it with an available goods cube.

If no goods cube exists, no ships may be purchased

### Office (cost: 8 coins)
At the end of phase 2, the player may draw a new goods card, regardless of which action they took in Phase 2

### Docker (cost: 12 coins)
The player may exchange an extra goods cube in Phase 1, regardless of the action chosen.  This may be performed as soon as this card has been purchased.

## Trade agreement (cost: 11 coins)
When a delivery occurs by the owner of this card, they receive 2 coins

> Example: The owner of two "trade agreement" special cards will receive four additional gold coins each time he scores instead of two.

# Game end

The game ends immediately hen the card draw is exhausted.  The player with the most coins wins.  

Special cards purchased during the game do not count towards victory.
